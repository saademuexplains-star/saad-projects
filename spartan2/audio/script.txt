You're seven when the soldiers come for you… and your mother isn't allowed to cry.
They take you to a camp, shave your head, take your shoes, and give you one thin cloak… for the whole year.
You sleep on reeds you ripped from the river with your bare hands, and you wake up starving… because they starve you on purpose.
They want you to steal. So one night, you grab a fox from a farm… but a guard walks in, and you hide it under your cloak.
While he questions you, the fox starts eating into your stomach… and you don't make a sound.
Because you've seen what happens to boys who get caught. They're whipped at the altar in front of the whole city… until the stone turns red.
You survive all of it. So at eighteen, they hand you a knife and send you into the night… to hunt slaves who did nothing wrong.
And at thirty, you're finally the man who walks into a house… and takes someone's seven year old son.
