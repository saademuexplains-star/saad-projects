You're twelve… and your only job is to taste the king's food before he does.
Every bite. Every cup. If you live, he eats. If you drop… he doesn't.
The boy before you lasted four months. You've lasted three years… and no one knows how.
Then one night, at the great feast, the king's wine tastes wrong. Bitter. Like crushed almonds.
The whole hall is watching you. Except the queen. She's staring at the king's cup… waiting.
Spit it out, and you're accusing the queen. You'll hang by morning.
So you smile… and you drink.
The queen leans forward… waiting for you to fall.
But you don't.
Because every morning for three years, you've swallowed one drop of poison. Just enough to survive tonight.
You turn to the queen. “My lady… this wine is too fine for a servant. The rest is yours.”
The king laughs and raises his cup. “Drink, my love!”
And now… the whole hall is watching her.
