**v2 in progress (9 Nov).** The user's second review of the delivered v1: hand/arm glitches still visible ("100 OF
TIMES IVE METIONED THIS THING"), the name "Coca-Cola" on screen only a fraction of a second, and the voice cut too
rigidly ("he ends one word and other appear instantly"). All three are fixed in code (commit b158b2c on

Page `_render/video/main20.html`: **3264 frames** (54.40 s, 60 fps). The shots keep their old times (timed to the tight
voice); `twarp_coca.js` (generated by `python3 -I _tools/twarp.py build`) gives the old time of every frame, and
`ENG.tOf(n)` reads it. The voice is `_vo18/vo_nat.wav` (the take with its own pauses, 52.13 s; also built by twarp.py).
Fonts must be installed first: `mkdir -p ~/.fonts && cp _render/fonts/*.ttf ~/.fonts/ && fc-cache -f`.
SFX: `_sfx18/wav/*.wav` (gitignored; session ids in `_sfx18/sessions.txt`, flow MfGFpNLswxYkaOy8BAHR; the environment
allows elevenlabsusercontent.com).
