A sword slices open your chest, and the doctors give you the only painkiller they have, morphine, and it works so well that you can never stop taking it. Twenty years after the American Civil War, you're a pharmacist in Atlanta, a respected man with a secret, desperate to find a substitute. So you start mixing medicines in your back room, and you find two strange ingredients: leaves of the coca plant, the same plant cocaine comes from, and a nut packed with caffeine. You blend them with wine and sell it as a cure for headaches, nerves, and even morphine addiction. When the city bans alcohol, you swap the wine for sweet brown syrup, mix it with fizzy water, and sell it as a brain tonic, five cents a glass. It never cures you. But when you die two years later, still hooked on morphine, after selling your share of the business, you never find out what your drink becomes. Today it's sold in almost every country on earth, and it's called Coca-Cola.
