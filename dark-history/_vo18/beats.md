# Coca-Cola: beat sheet (trimmed VO 47.320 s, 2840 frames at 60 fps; times from words.txt)

Locations: the battlefield at Columbus (night, 1865), the back room (lamplight, 1885-88), the street outside the
shop (dusk, 1885, one short wide), the soda fountain (day, 1886); an antique world map for the finale. Faces or the
story object big nearly all the time. One light source per shot. The name "Coca-Cola" appears only on the last line.
Loop: the last frames push into the drink's rising bubbles, which become the embers rising over the burning town in
frame 0.

on every transition, foley for every action (sabre, cry, bottle, pestle, pour, newspaper, kettle, fizz, coin, calendar,
lamp out), character vocals (cry, gasp, groan, breath).
