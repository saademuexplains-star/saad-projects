## The three corrections

### 1. Hands and arms: "they look very off ... make them cleaner and finer so they dont look like cutted pngs"

| What looked wrong | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| A glowing halo round the forearms over a blanket or a basin | The arms were redrawn over the furniture with `ST.armsOver`, which lights them in their own `C.lit` pass. The rim then ran round each arm's own outline, including its inner edge against the body. | `ST.behind(P, id, svg)` (stage.js): the furniture drawn after the body is masked out wherever the forearms and hands are, so they stay in the body's single light pass. `armsOver` is no longer used. |
| A hand on a prop lit on its own (the same halo round the fist) | `handsLit` lit the detached fist in its own pass. | `ST.litWith(P, L, P.svg + prop + P.handR, ext)`: body, prop and fist in ONE pass; `ext` widens the light's box to take in the prop. |
| The hand's flat top corner sticking out past the wrist when the hand bends | The wrist was a straight capsule laid along the forearm. | rig3.js, clean rig only: `wristBridge()` curves from inside the cuff into the hand's own line, and the hand's top dissolves into it (`joinWrist` in `handV2`). |
| A glassy bubble on light cuffs | The half-transparent "open end of the sleeve" ellipse, drawn when the forearm points at the camera. | rig3.js: not drawn when `RIG_CRISP` is on. |
| Fists from the back looked like paws; a strip of bare wrist showed above the water | Pose choices. | Hands resting on something use `relaxed` with the fingers over its edge, plus a soft contact shadow. Arms that go into water reach far enough for the cuffs to go under. The water is split at the waterline (far half under the arms, near half over them), opaque and milky, and the near half of the bowl's rim lip is drawn over the arms. |

The clean-rig changes only apply when `window.RIG_CRISP = true`, so old pages render exactly as before. The Coca-Cola
look test was re-rendered with them: its bottle, pestle and glass grips are unchanged and its wrists are cleaner.

### 2. Character detailing: "their clothes and hairs look uneven and a bit not detailed and clean"

- **Cloth weave (rig3.js):**
  - `weave: { kind: 'twill' | 'linen', op }` on a character lays a fine thread texture on the torso and both sleeves,
    under their shading.
  - Patterns `rgWvTwill` / `rgWvLinen` use neutral dark and light threads, so they work on any colour.
  - Keep it subtle: op 0.12 to 0.14 on dark wool. Stronger reads as stripes.
- **Tailoring (tailor.js):**
  - `T.jacket({ weave: 'twill', weaveOp, velvet: true })`: twill on the lapels, pick-stitching along the lapel edges, a
    buttonhole in the left lapel, and a darker velvet upper collar with its seam. Use `velvet` on frock coats only,
    never on a white cotton jacket.
  - `T.vest({ col, figure, chain })` (new): a silk waistcoat seen in the jacket's opening, with a fine woven figure,
    lit edges, three buttons and an optional gold watch chain. Layer it after `T.shirt` and before `T.collar` and
    `T.jacket`.
  - `T.collar`: the bow is shaded silk with folds and a knot with creases; the stand collar has a jaw shadow and
    topstitching.
  - `T.shirt({ pleats })`: pressed pleats (a shadow plus a lit edge), clipped to the shirt's opening when the shirt has
    its own colour. Before, they ran on below the waistcoat onto the coat.
- **Nightgown (castT1.js `GOWN`):** a pin-tucked yoke with a stitched seam, gathers below it, a lace-edged neck frill
  with eyelets, and a silk drawstring bow.
- **Hair:** the new hair kit, `_render/lib/hairkit.js`. See section 3.

### 3. Hair: "hairs still look very off and cutted at some points ... not matching"

- **Light the hair with the head (the "not matching").** Hair drawn outside the figure's light pass stays flat while
  the face takes the key, rim and face light. Draw back hair, body and front hair (plus any prop and fist) through ONE
  `C.lit` with `Q.litHair` (castT1.js, below).
- **No straight cuts (the "cutted").**
  - A woman's hair covers the whole ear and rounds back behind it.
  - Sideburns taper to a rounded end.
  - A bald man's fringe thins out at both ends and lies over the side of the head above the ear as well as behind it.
  - A parting is a soft blurred groove, never a drawn line.
- **One gradient per head**, shared by every piece (front, back, plait) in the same coordinates, so the joins don't
  change tone.
- **A few wide locks** with faint lines between them, not many thin strokes. No fly-aways or hairline ticks: they read
  as scratches.
- **The styles (`G.HAIRKIT`):**
  - `HK.centrePart({ fx, col, id, light, braid: 1 | -1 | 0, ribbon })`: a woman, sleek over the ears, with a night
    plait made of close-set pointed lenses over a solid core.
  - `HK.sidePart({ fx, col, id, light, part })`: an 1840s man, swept across in a wave, with tapered sideburns.
  - `HK.baldFringe({ fx, col, id, light })`: an old man, a bald crown with soft grey hair at the sides and back.
  - Each returns `{ back, front }`. Set the rig's `hairStyle: 'bald'` and load `hairkit.js` after `stage.js`. The
    shapes are drawn on the base head and mapped onto each morphed head with the rig's `faceP`, so pass the
    character's `fx`.
- **Review hair and hands at 2.5x** (render the page with a device scale of 2.5 and clip the heads). These flaws did not
  show on 1080 crops.

## Using it in a new cast and new shots

The page loads `../lib/hairkit.js` after `stage.js` (see `lookT1.html`). In the cast file:

```js
const T = G.TAILOR, HK = G.HAIRKIT;
// draw head-local art where the rig puts the head; light hair, body and anything held in ONE pass
Q.headArt = (P, o, svg) => { const s = o.s || 1, b = o.breath || 0, sx = Math.round(s * (1 - b * 0.0015) * 1e5) / 1e5, sy = Math.round(s * (1 + b * 0.004) * 1e5) / 1e5, hsc = o.headScale ?? 1;
  return `<g transform="translate(${o.x || 0},${o.y || 0}) rotate(${o.sway || 0}) scale(${sx},${sy})"><g transform="translate(0,${P.D.headY + (1 - hsc) * 110}) rotate(${o.tilt || 0}) scale(${hsc})">${svg}</g></g>`; };
Q.litHair = (P, o, art, L, extra = '', ext = null) => { const s = o.s || 1, E = [o.x - 340 * s, o.y - 1520 * s, o.x + 340 * s, o.y + 20 * s];
  const e2 = ext ? [Math.min(E[0], ext[0]), Math.min(E[1], ext[1]), Math.max(E[2], ext[2]), Math.max(E[3], ext[3])] : E;
  return G.STAGE.litWith(P, L, (art && art.back ? Q.headArt(P, o, art.back) : '') + P.svg + (art && art.front ? Q.headArt(P, o, art.front) : '') + extra, e2); };

const FX = { w: 1.06, len: 0.96 /* ... */ };
Q.doctor = mk({ kind: 'man', tunic: '#30402c', sleeve: '#2d3c29', tailored: true, hairStyle: 'bald', fx: FX,
  weave: { kind: 'twill', op: 0.12 },
  costume: T.shirt({ col: '#f3efe6', placket: true, pleats: true }) + T.vest({ col: '#5c2622' }) + T.collar({ type: 'stand' })
         + T.jacket({ col: '#30402c', bkind: 'horn', weave: 'twill', weaveOp: 0.12, velvet: true }) }, { expr: 'neutral' });
Q.doctorArt = () => HK.sidePart({ fx: FX, col: '#5e4026', id: 'dhr', light: 1 });   // light: the key light's side
```

In a shot:

```js
const P = ST.up(Q.doctor(Object.assign(o, { /* face, armR, armL */ })), o);
let chars = Q.litHair(P, o, Q.doctorArt(), L);                                     // hair, body, hands: one pass
chars += ST.behind(P, 'tbl1', table);                                               // furniture over the body, not over the arms
// a held prop: body + hair + prop + its detached fist in one pass, the light box widened for the prop
chars = Q.litHair(P, o, Q.doctorArt(), L, prop + P.handR, [x0, y0, x1, y1]);
```
