Your King kneels before you in a dark wooden booth every night to confess his sins, knowing the law says if you ever repeat a single word he tells you, your tongue will be cut out and you will be burned alive. You carry the darkest crimes of the empire inside your head in silence, until one evening the King coldly confesses that at dawn tomorrow, he is ordering his army to burn an entire rebellious village to the ground, including the street where your own mother lives. Ten minutes after the King leaves, his young kitchen boy kneels on the other side of the screen trembling and confesses that he bought rat poison to kill the King, but lost his nerve and slides the glass vial through the grate for you to destroy. You tell the boy his sins are forgiven, pour the entire vial into the sacred communion wine the King takes before sleep, and watch the tyrant choke to death at your feet, trading your own soul to save a thousand innocent lives.
