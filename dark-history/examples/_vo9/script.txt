A village doctor ties you to a wooden chair, cuts your healthy arm wide open with a sharp knife, and rubs thick yellow pus from a sick farm girl's blister straight into your bleeding wound. Outside your window, every third person on your street is dying from a deadly plague that covers their skin in painful sores, yet you still try to pull your arm back as the doctor forces a nasty sickness from a farm cow straight into your blood. For three long days you shake in bed as your body burns with a high fever, and the very moment you finally get better, the doctor ties you back down and does something crazy, he scrapes the real, deadly plague straight off a dying man's skin and rubs it deep into your cut just to see if you drop dead. The cow sickness in your blood destroys the plague before a single sore can form, the doctor slices your arm and poisons you with the plague twenty more times just to be sure you can't die, and that insane test creates the world's very first vaccine.
