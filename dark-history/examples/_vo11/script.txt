He gives you a golden crown identical to his own, dresses you in his finest silk robes, and forces you to ride in his royal carriage to catch the arrows meant for his chest. Every trip outside the castle walls is a game of Russian roulette. Until one afternoon, you're riding through a mountain pass while the real king rides disguised as a common guard right beside your window. You look ahead at the stone bridge and spot barrels of black gunpowder stacked beneath the arches. You glance up at the cliffside and see the king's own brother holding a flaming torch, staring dead at you from the ridge. He wired the bridge to blow, and he's waiting to see if you pull the emergency brake. Your mouth goes completely dry and you don't dare yell for the driver to stop, because you know what happens to decoys who ruin an ambush. The brother's mercenaries will burn your entire village to ash before sundown. So you lean out the carriage window, point at the front wheel, and order the disguised king to gallop ahead and secure the bridge. The king spurs his horse onto the stones just as the barrels explode, sending him plunging into the canyon in a ball of fire.
