You're 8 when the King brings you into the palace, dresses you in silk, and makes you the Prince's best friend. There's only one catch: the Prince's blood is considered sacred, so no adult is allowed to lay a hand on him. Instead, whenever the Prince refuses to study or breaks a window, the guards drag you into the classroom. The Prince sits in the corner eating grapes while the royal tutor pulls out a heavy leather rod. He swings it across your bare back, and you don't try to act tough—you scream as loud as your lungs will let you. Because you know the sick rule of your job: the tutor isn't allowed to stop hitting you until the Prince feels guilty enough to cry. Some days the Prince just laughs, and you bleed onto the marble floor for an hour. You survive the beatings and finally turn 18, thinking the nightmare is over—until the Prince has a spoiled son of his own, hands you a bag of gold to find a poor kid on the streets.
