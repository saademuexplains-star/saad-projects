The bottle on your kitchen shelf has one word printed on it, a word that almost every country on earth will one day ban, but right now it's the most trusted cough medicine money can buy, the label swears it's safer than morphine, and your child hasn't stopped coughing for a week. So you pour one spoonful, and like magic, the cough stops and your child sleeps like an angel. But the next night, one spoonful isn't enough, so you give two, and soon you're taking it too, every morning and every night, until one day the bottle runs out. Your hands start shaking, your whole body aches, and from the next room, you hear your child screaming for it, while at the pharmacy, the line is full of parents with the same shaking hands, and by the time doctors admit what they've done, it's too late, because that one word printed on the bottle was Heroin.
