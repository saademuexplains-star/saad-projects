Your Emperor blinds you with hot iron, gives you a golden harp, and makes you play inside his secret war room because a blind man can't read his battle maps. Every song is a game of survival, until one night your bandages slip and you realize your eyes have healed just enough to see blurred shapes. You look up mid-song and see the Emperor's own brother pouring poison into the royal wine, and when you accidentally flinch, he freezes with his hand on his dagger, realizing you can see and waiting to see if you warn the Emperor. You force a blank stare and keep playing without missing a single note, the Emperor chokes to death, and the brother makes you his personal war-room musician.
