Your king pays you a fortune to read his future in the stars, gives you a tower full of golden instruments, and warns you that one wrong prediction costs your head. The last astrologer's head still hangs above the castle gate. Every night is a gamble, until you foretell that his favorite lady will die within eight days, and suddenly you're trapped: if she lives, you're a fraud, and if she dies, you're a murderer. On the eighth day, she does. The king summons you at midnight, sure you cursed her, and you see his guards by the open window staring dead at you, waiting for his signal to throw you out of it. He smiles and asks, "You know everything, so tell me, when will you die?" You bow and answer, "Exactly three days before Your Majesty." He turns pale, waves the guards away, and for the rest of his life, he makes sure you never even catch a cold.
