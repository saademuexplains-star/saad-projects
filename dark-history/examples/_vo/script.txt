Your king hands you a scroll for the enemy warlord, and pays your mother in advance for your funeral. When you reach his throne room, the warlord can't read—so he presses a bloody axe to your throat and tells you to read it out loud. You break the wax seal, look at the first line, and your stomach drops. Your king didn't write a peace treaty. He wrote two pages calling the warlord a fat, bald coward, and drew a picture of him crying. Your hands shake, and you don't dare read a single word. Because you've seen what happens to messengers who ruin his mood. They're pinned to the floor and have molten silver poured down their throats. So you look dead into his eyes, force a smile, and make up a poem about how handsome and terrifying he is.
