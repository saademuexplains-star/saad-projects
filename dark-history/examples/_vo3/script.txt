Every meal is a game of Russian roulette, until one night at a royal feast, you take a bite of roasted meat and your tongue instantly goes numb. You look up across the table, and see the King's own brother staring dead at you with his hand on his dagger. He poisoned the plate, and he's waiting to see if you warn the King. Your throat burns like swallowed glass, sweat pours down your neck, and you don't dare cough or drop your spoon. Because you know what happens to tasters who ruin a coup. The traitors lock your entire family inside your house and burn it to the ground. So you look at the King, force a smile, and tell him the meat is delicious. The King laughs, takes a massive bite off the same plate, and chokes to death on the table. While the guards panic, the King's brother slips an antidote into your pocket, puts the crown on his own head and makes you his personal taster.
